# About This Study

## Purpose

**My Journey Through Vedanta** is a personal study journal built from handwritten notes, reflections, and continued study of Vedanta, especially through *Vedanta Treatise* by Sri A. Parthasarathy.

The purpose of this site is not to reproduce the source book. It is to document a learning journey: what was heard, what was written down, how the ideas were understood, how they connect to the broader Vedantic tradition, and how they may be applied in everyday life.

## AI-Assisted Study Disclaimer

This is a **personal study journal** based on handwritten notes and the author's evolving understanding of Vedanta. Artificial intelligence (AI) is used to help transcribe, organize, structure, expand, and present the notes in a readable study-book format.

AI-generated explanations can contain errors, oversimplifications, or interpretations that may not precisely represent the original teachings. Where supplementary ideas from the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Advaita Vedanta, or other sources are added, they are intended as study context rather than as a substitute for the original teaching.

Readers are therefore encouraged to study the original texts and teachings for authoritative understanding and to learn from qualified teachers where possible.

This site is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official publication of** Sri A. Parthasarathy, Vedanta Academy, Vedanta USA, Gautamji, or any organization referenced here.

## How the Notes Are Developed

Each handwritten page is treated as a source document. The study workflow is:

1. **Transcribe the handwritten notes.**
2. **Flag unclear words or concepts rather than guessing.**
3. **Confirm ambiguities with the note author.**
4. **Organize the ideas into a coherent study structure.**
5. **Expand the concepts in accessible language.**
6. **Add simple examples and practical applications.**
7. **Add broader Vedantic context where it genuinely helps.**
8. **Preserve a clear distinction between personal notes, explanation, and supplementary context.**

The recurring study pattern is:

> **From My Notes → Expanded Understanding → Simple Example → Deeper Vedantic Connection → Practical Application → Reflection**

## A Living Study Book

This site is intended to grow gradually with continued study. It should therefore be read as an evolving personal notebook rather than a finished commentary on Vedanta.

Corrections, deeper understanding, and revised interpretations may be incorporated as the study progresses.
