**Status:** Handwritten notes clarified and expanded

## What I captured from the Preface

The Preface introduces several foundational ideas:

- Living is an **art, skill, and technique**.
- Life is a **stream of experiences**.
- Our peace or disturbance often depends on the outcomes life gives us.
- Vedanta teaches us to reduce that dependence while remaining fully active in life.
- Human beings often appear divided between those who are active/productive/prosperous and those who are peaceful/happy.
- Vedanta seeks to unite **dynamic action with inner peace**.
- Knowledge of the unknown is approached through what is already known.
- Practical Vedanta examines the human personality, the three common states of consciousness, different personality tendencies, and four broad paths of development.
- The journey of the seeker progresses through **dispassion**, **meditation**, and finally **Self-realization**.
