# My Journey Through Vedanta

> A personal study book of notes, understanding, examples, reflections, and practical application inspired by *Vedanta Treatise* by A. Parthasarathy.

This site is not intended to reproduce the source book. It is a growing record of my own study journey: what I heard, what I wrote down, how I understood it, and how I can apply it to everyday life.

## How each topic is organized

Every concept is developed through the same study pattern:

1. **From My Notes** — the original idea captured in my handwritten notes.
2. **Expanded Understanding** — a fuller explanation in simple language.
3. **Simple Example** — an everyday illustration.
4. **Deeper Vedantic Connection** — supplementary context from the broader Vedantic tradition where helpful.
5. **Practical Application** — how the concept can be observed or practiced in daily life.
6. **Reflection** — questions for personal contemplation.
