# Privacy

This is a personal study notebook. I collect as little as I can, and I don't sell or share data with advertisers. There are no ads and no accounts.

## What may be collected

**Cloudflare Web Analytics.** Counts visits and page views to understand which pages are read. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not build a profile of you.

This site may also use **Google Analytics** to see how many people visit, which pages are read and for how long, and roughly where visitors are from (country or city level), along with browser and device type. It sets cookies in your browser.

Google Analytics loads **only if you click Accept** on the cookie banner. If you decline, it never loads. Your choice is stored in your own browser, and nowhere else.

To change your choice at any time, use [Change cookie settings](#__consent).

## Other things to know

- **Reflection answer boxes.** Some reflection articles let you type answers to the questions. What you type is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so a refresh does not lose it. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. You can remove it with the "Clear my answers" button on the page or by clearing your browser's site data.
- Links to other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own policy.
- My main website, [pavanturlapati.com](https://pavanturlapati.com/privacy/), has its own privacy page.
