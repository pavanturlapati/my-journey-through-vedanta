# Privacy

This is a personal study notebook. I collect as little as I can, and I don't sell or share data with advertisers. There are no ads and no accounts.

## What may be collected

This site may use **Google Analytics** to see how many people visit, which pages are read and for how long, and roughly where visitors are from (country or city level), along with browser and device type. It sets cookies in your browser.

Google Analytics loads **only if you click Accept** on the cookie banner. If you decline, it never loads. Your choice is stored in your own browser, and nowhere else.

To change your choice at any time, use [Change cookie settings](#__consent).

## Other things to know

- Links to other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own policy.
- My main website, [pavanturlapati.com](https://pavanturlapati.com/privacy/), has its own privacy page.
