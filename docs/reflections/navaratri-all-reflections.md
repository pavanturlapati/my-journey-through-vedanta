# Navaratri 2026: All Reflections in One Place

*Part of the [Navaratri series](navaratri.md).*

This page gathers the reflection questions from every published article in the series, so you can work through them in one place. The same boxes also appear at the end of each article. Your answers are shared between the two, so what you type here shows up there, and the other way round.

Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. Each day has its own print-friendly page and AI prompt button. The questions are suggestions only, not professional, legal or religious advice.

## Introduction: Nine Nights, Two Ways of Understanding

Read the article: [Navaratri: Nine Nights, Two Ways of Understanding](navaratri-nine-nights-vedanta-and-tradition.md)

1. **What do I hope to take from these nine nights?** What would make this Navaratri meaningful for me, in worship, in reflection, or both?
2. **What is my Mahishasura?** Which inner tendency, such as ego, inertia, or an unchecked desire, most often wins over my better judgement?
3. **What can I sustain for nine days?** What small discipline, practice or act of attention could I keep up, even when it is not exciting?
4. **What do worship and inquiry each give me?** How do devotion and questioning support or challenge each other in my life?
5. **What will I carry forward on Vijayadashami?** What is one practical change I would like to still be living in a month?

## Day 1: Shailaputri, What Is the Foundation of My Life?

Read the article: [Day 1 — Shailaputri](navaratri-day-1-shailaputri-foundation.md)

1. **What is the foundation of my life today?** What do I currently rely on most when making decisions or facing difficulty?
2. **What would I want to remain unchanged if my circumstances changed?** Consider qualities, commitments, relationships, or ways of responding—not only possessions or roles.
3. **What gives that something its importance?** Does it matter because it brings joy, helps others, gives me identity, earns recognition, or something else?
4. **What remains when a role or achievement changes?** What do I notice about myself in that moment?
5. **What am I still searching for?** Can I allow the question to remain open without rushing to manufacture an answer?

## Day 2: Brahmacharini, What Does Steady Effort Look Like?

Read the article: [Day 2 — Brahmacharini](navaratri-day-2-brahmacharini-steady-effort.md)

1. **What am I committed to over the long term?** What aim or practice would I be glad to still be working on a year from now?
2. **What does my steady effort look like in daily life?** What small action do I repeat, and does it happen only when I feel motivated?
3. **What tests my patience?** When do I feel like giving up, and what usually happens next?
4. **How is my mind during effort: calm or agitated?** Which of serenity, gentleness, silence or self-control do I most need to develop?
5. **What do I hope this effort will give me?** If I achieved it, would I feel complete, or would I still be asking a deeper question?

## Day 3: Chandraghanta, What Does Calm Courage Look Like?

Read the article: [Day 3 — Chandraghanta](navaratri-day-3-chandraghanta-calm-courage.md)

1. **What am I afraid of right now?** Name it plainly, whether large or small.
2. **How do I behave when things become unsettled?** What do I tend to do first: freeze, react, withdraw, or take charge?
3. **What helps me stay steady?** Which people, habits, breaths or words bring me back to a calm centre?
4. **What is my fear showing me that I depend on?** If I look beneath the fear, what am I afraid of losing?
5. **What would calm courage look like for me this week?** What is one small, concrete way to be ready without being reactive?

## Day 4: Kushmanda, Where Does My Light Come From?

Read the article: [Day 4 — Kushmanda](navaratri-day-4-kushmanda-source-of-light.md)

1. **What am I creating or beginning in my life right now?** It may be small: a habit, a relationship, a project, a meal, a conversation.
2. **Does my effort come from fullness or from strain?** What changes in me when I act without anxiety about the result?
3. **Where does my light come from?** Which people, teachers, circumstances or abilities made it possible for me to do what I do well?
4. **Whom or what do I need to thank?** Is there a source of light in my life that I have never acknowledged?
5. **How could I be a gentle source of light for someone this week?** What is one small, concrete act of warmth, encouragement or help?
