# Navaratri 2026: All Reflections in One Place

*Part of the [Navaratri series](navaratri.md).*

This page gathers the reflection questions from every published article in the series, so you can work through them in one place. The same boxes also appear at the end of each article. Your answers are shared between the two, so what you type here shows up there, and the other way round.

Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. Each day has its own print-friendly page and AI prompt button. The questions are suggestions only, not professional, legal or religious advice.

## Introduction: Nine Nights, Two Ways of Understanding

Read the article: [Navaratri: Nine Nights, Two Ways of Understanding](navaratri-nine-nights-vedanta-and-tradition.md)

1. **What do I hope to take from these nine nights?** What would make this Navaratri meaningful for me, in worship, in reflection, or both?
2. **What is my Mahishasura?** Which inner tendency, such as ego, inertia, or an unchecked desire, most often wins over my better judgement?
3. **What can I sustain for nine days?** What small discipline, practice or act of attention could I keep up, even when it is not exciting?
4. **What do worship and inquiry each give me?** How do devotion and questioning support or challenge each other in my life?
5. **What will I carry forward on Vijayadashami?** What is one practical change I would like to still be living in a month?

## Day 1: Shailaputri, What Is the Foundation of My Life?

Read the article: [Day 1 — Shailaputri](navaratri-day-1-shailaputri-foundation.md)

1. **What is the foundation of my life today?** What do I currently rely on most when making decisions or facing difficulty?
2. **What would I want to remain unchanged if my circumstances changed?** Consider qualities, commitments, relationships, or ways of responding—not only possessions or roles.
3. **What gives that something its importance?** Does it matter because it brings joy, helps others, gives me identity, earns recognition, or something else?
4. **What remains when a role or achievement changes?** What do I notice about myself in that moment?
5. **What am I still searching for?** Can I allow the question to remain open without rushing to manufacture an answer?

## Day 2: Brahmacharini, What Does Steady Effort Look Like?

Read the article: [Day 2 — Brahmacharini](navaratri-day-2-brahmacharini-steady-effort.md)

1. **What am I committed to over the long term?** What aim or practice would I be glad to still be working on a year from now?
2. **What does my steady effort look like in daily life?** What small action do I repeat, and does it happen only when I feel motivated?
3. **What tests my patience?** When do I feel like giving up, and what usually happens next?
4. **How is my mind during effort: calm or agitated?** Which of serenity, gentleness, silence or self-control do I most need to develop?
5. **What do I hope this effort will give me?** If I achieved it, would I feel complete, or would I still be asking a deeper question?

## Day 3: Chandraghanta, What Does Calm Courage Look Like?

Read the article: [Day 3 — Chandraghanta](navaratri-day-3-chandraghanta-calm-courage.md)

1. **What am I afraid of right now?** Name it plainly, whether large or small.
2. **How do I behave when things become unsettled?** What do I tend to do first: freeze, react, withdraw, or take charge?
3. **What helps me stay steady?** Which people, habits, breaths or words bring me back to a calm centre?
4. **What is my fear showing me that I depend on?** If I look beneath the fear, what am I afraid of losing?
5. **What would calm courage look like for me this week?** What is one small, concrete way to be ready without being reactive?

<div class="reflection-journal" data-key="navaratri-day-3" data-title="Day 3 of Navaratri — Chandraghanta: What Does Calm Courage Look Like?" data-hints="a hard conversation, a health worry, speaking up at work, or “nothing in particular”|I tend to go quiet first and then overthink it|a slow breath, a short walk, talking it through with someone I trust|I seem to fear losing approval, or my sense of being capable|pausing for one breath before I reply when I feel tense"></div>

## Day 4: Kushmanda, Where Does My Light Come From?

Read the article: [Day 4 — Kushmanda](navaratri-day-4-kushmanda-source-of-light.md)

1. **What am I creating or beginning in my life right now?** It may be small: a habit, a relationship, a project, a meal, a conversation.
2. **Does my effort come from fullness or from strain?** What changes in me when I act without anxiety about the result?
3. **Where does my light come from?** Which people, teachers, circumstances or abilities made it possible for me to do what I do well?
4. **Whom or what do I need to thank?** Is there a source of light in my life that I have never acknowledged?
5. **How could I be a gentle source of light for someone this week?** What is one small, concrete act of warmth, encouragement or help?

## Day 5: Skandamata, Whom Am I Nurturing?

Read the article: [Day 5 — Skandamata](navaratri-day-5-skandamata-nurturing-care.md)

1. **Whom am I nurturing right now?** It may be a child, a parent, a student, a friend, a plant, or even a part of myself.
2. **Who nurtured me?** Whose care helped me become who I am, and have I ever said thank you?
3. **Does my care come with conditions?** Do I quietly expect gratitude, obedience or a certain outcome in return?
4. **Where do I find it hard to let go?** What would it look like to support someone's growth without steering it?
5. **How do I care for myself?** What is one small, kind thing I could do for myself this week, so that I can keep caring for others?

<div class="reflection-journal" data-key="navaratri-day-5" data-title="Day 5 of Navaratri — Skandamata: Whom Am I Nurturing?" data-hints="my children, an elderly parent, a new colleague, a friendship I want to keep up|a grandparent, a teacher, a neighbour who helped when I needed it|I sometimes hope to be thanked, and I notice it when I'm not|letting a grown child make their own choices|resting properly, a walk, saying no to one extra task"></div>

## Day 6: Katyayani, What Am I Called to Stand Up For?

Read the article: [Day 6 — Katyayani](navaratri-day-6-katyayani-righteous-resolve.md)

1. **What do I stand for?** Which two or three values would I find hard to give up?
2. **Where have I stayed silent when I should have spoken?** What held me back?
3. **What is my inner opponent?** Is it anger, craving, fear, laziness or something else that most often pulls me off course?
4. **Can I be firm without being harsh?** What would it look like to act clearly, with a lotus in one hand?
5. **What is one small, brave step I can take this week?** It might be a conversation, a boundary or a habit.

## Day 7: Kalaratri, What Am I Afraid to Look At?

Read the article: [Day 7 — Kalaratri](navaratri-day-7-kalaratri-facing-the-dark.md)

1. **What am I afraid to look at?** It might be a decision, a conversation, a loss, or something about myself.
2. **What story have I built around it?** How much of what I imagine is known, and how much is guessed?
3. **What has fear cost me?** What have I avoided, delayed or not said because of it?
4. **What helps me stay steady in the dark?** A prayer, a person, a breath, a routine?
5. **What is one small light I can switch on this week?** One step toward looking at it, even for a minute.

## Day 8: Mahagauri, What Can I Wash Away and Let Go?

Read the article: [Day 8 — Mahagauri](navaratri-day-8-mahagauri-letting-go.md)

1. **What am I carrying that I no longer need?** It might be an old grudge, a habit, a possession, or a belief about myself.
2. **What has the "dust" of effort and struggle left on me?** Where am I tired, hardened or self-critical?
3. **What would it mean to forgive, myself or someone else?** What could be washed away by that?
4. **What does simplicity look like for me right now?** What could I remove from my day, my space or my mind?
5. **What is one small act of cleansing I can do this week?** It might be a conversation, a tidy-up, a day of rest or a prayer.

## Day 9: Siddhidatri, What Does Fulfilment Really Mean?

Read the article: [Day 9 — Siddhidatri](navaratri-day-9-siddhidatri-fulfilment.md)

1. **What does fulfilment mean to me?** If I imagine feeling truly complete, what is present in that picture?
2. **What have I already received?** Which things in my life did I once hope for and now take for granted?
3. **Which "powers" or achievements do I chase?** What am I hoping they will give me?
4. **What has changed in me over these nine days?** Even a small shift counts.
5. **What will I carry forward?** One quality, practice or question from this series that I would like to keep.

## Day 10: Vijayadashami, What Does Victory Really Mean?

Read the article: [Day 10 — Vijayadashami](navaratri-day-10-vijayadashami-victory.md)

1. **What does victory mean to me?** When I picture a real win, what is in that picture?
2. **Which of my "ten heads" is the strongest right now?** Is it anger, pride, envy, greed, laziness or something else?
3. **What is something I "won" that left me feeling smaller?** What would I do differently?
4. **What did the last nine days show me?** Which day's question stayed with me most, and why?
5. **What is the one thing I want to begin, or keep going, now that the nights are over?** What would a first step look like?
