# Navaratri — Nine Nights, Nine Reflections

**Series started:** October 11, 2026 (first day of Navaratri)  
**Progress:** Day 4 of 9 published  
**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

This page is the home for my Navaratri reflections. Each day I look at one form of the Divine Mother through the lens of Vedanta and tradition, and I add the new article here as soon as it is published. Bookmark this page to follow the series.

Navaratri, "nine nights", is a festival devoted to the Divine Mother in her nine forms. It is a time of worship, discipline and inner reflection, and I read it alongside the Vedantic idea that the one Self appears in many forms.

## Day 1 — Shailaputri

Shailaputri, the "daughter of the mountain", is worshipped on the first day. She stands for steadiness and for the foundation on which every later step depends. This reflection asks what it means to begin the inner journey on firm ground.

## Day 2 — Brahmacharini

Brahmacharini, the form of the Goddess who walks barefoot with a rosary and a water pot, is worshipped on the second day. This article covers customs, the story of Parvati's penance, the meaning of her symbols, and *tapas* as the steady training of the mind.

## Day 3 — Chandraghanta

Chandraghanta, the form with a crescent moon like a bell on her forehead, is worshipped on the third day. This article covers the stories of the wedding and the battle, her symbols, and fearlessness as a calm, steady mind.
