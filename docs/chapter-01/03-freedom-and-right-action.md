# Human Freedom and Right Action

## Human Beings Have Freedom of Choice

<div class="handwritten-note">
All other creatures than humans have a computerized, pre-set programme of action. A tiger cannot become a vegetarian, nor can a cow become a meat-eater. Humans alone are faced with the dilemma of choosing their action.
</div>

<div class="ai-note">
The example contrasts instinct-dominated behavior with the human capacity for conscious choice. That freedom is both a privilege and a difficulty. When choice exists, confusion can exist. Vedanta seeks to educate the individual so that freedom of choice is guided by discrimination rather than merely by impulse.
</div>

## Right Action: Prosperity with Peace

<div class="handwritten-note">
Vedanta equips you with the knowledge to make the <strong>right choice of action</strong>. Following that course, you enjoy <strong>prosperity with peace of mind</strong>.
</div>

<div class="ai-note">
The purpose of Vedanta is not merely philosophical discussion. Knowledge should influence how a person thinks, chooses and acts. The desired outcome is not prosperity at the cost of peace, nor peace achieved by withdrawing from productive life, but <strong>prosperity with peace of mind</strong>.
</div>
