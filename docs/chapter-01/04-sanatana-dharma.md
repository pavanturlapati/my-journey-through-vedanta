# Sanatana Dharma and the Divine Self

## Eternal Principles

<div class="handwritten-note">
Vedanta is known as <strong>Sanatana Dharma</strong>, meaning Eternal Principles. <strong>Divinity is the core of every person.</strong> Vedanta helps you identify your divine Self and attain your absolute state of peace and bliss. This is the goal of every religion. The word religion means that which binds one to the origin. In Sanskrit, <em>yoga</em> means to unite with the Self.
</div>

<div class="ai-note">
This passage presents religion at its deepest level not simply as ritual, membership or belief, but as a movement back toward one's essential nature.

The phrase <strong>“Divinity is the core of every person”</strong> is central. The teaching does not suggest that spiritual practice manufactures divinity. Divinity is understood as intrinsic to the Self; identification with body, mind and intellect obscures its recognition.

In this framework, Vedanta provides the knowledge by which the seeker investigates that mistaken identification. Peace and bliss are therefore connected with discovery of one's essential nature rather than merely with favorable external experiences.
</div>
